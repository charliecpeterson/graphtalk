Bike share, 2025: five questions

One plot per question. Synthetic data for a fictional city: 149,495 trips, 80 stations, 365 days. Hover any mark for its numbers.

1 · Who rides when?

Members ride the commute; casual riders ride weekend afternoons

Average trips per hour slot, by weekday and hour of day. Same color scale in both panels.

2 · How does weather change riding?

Riding rises to about 22 °C, and rain takes 40 to 70 trips off a typical day

Mean trips per day by 4 °C temperature band; rainy day = 1 mm or more. Marker size is the number of days in the band; bands under 3 days are dropped.

3 · Which days stand out?

The ten biggest days are all weekends, from June to October

Trips per day, one cell per day. Outlined cells are the ten busiest, numbered by rank.

4 · Did e-bikes gain share?

Every neighborhood gained e-bike share, yet the city total slipped

Share of trips on e-bikes by start neighborhood: Jan–Jun (open dot) to Jul–Dec (arrow tip). Harbor Flats only has second-half trips, and its low share pulls the total down.

5 · Where do bikes pile up?

Downtown and Harbor Flats fill up; University, Hillcrest and Greenway drain

Net flow per station: trips ending there minus trips starting there. Position is the station's (synthetic) location; size shows how large the imbalance is. Trips with no recorded end station count only as starts.
